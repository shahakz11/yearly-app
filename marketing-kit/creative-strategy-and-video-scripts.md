## 1. Marketing Psychology & Mental Models (Scored via PLFS)

```
Formula: PLFS = (Leverage + Fit + Speed + Ethics) − Implementation Cost  [Range: -5 to +15]
```

### Model 1: Loss Aversion & Regret Minimization
- **PLFS:** `+14` (High-confidence lever — Apply immediately)
- **Why it works:** The psychological pain of forgetting an anniversary or looking like an uncaring partner is 2.5x more intense than the pleasure of buying flowers. "Saving yourself from disaster" drives instant impulsive downloads.
- **Behavior targeted:** Immediate app installation and calendar permission grant.
- **Where to apply:** Hook & opening scene tension (the moment of near-disaster).
- **Ethical guardrail:** Keep the tone humorous and relatable, not toxic or manipulative.

### Model 2: Friction Elimination & The Default Effect
- **PLFS:** `+13` (High-confidence lever — Apply immediately)
- **Why it works:** Forgetfulness ("שכחנות") and low-effort procrastination ("עצלנות") cause people to delay until it's too late. When the solution is reduced from a 15-minute search chore to a 1-tap notification, resistance drops to zero.
- **Behavior targeted:** Tap notification → 1-click bouquet purchase.
- **Where to apply:** Mid-video product demo transition (notification pop-up → "Send flowers" → Done).

### Model 3: Tension-and-Relief Comic Reversal (Twist Ending)
- **PLFS:** `+12` (High-confidence lever)
- **Why it works:** Brain dopamine spikes when a stressful social situation abruptly resolves into romantic delight. The contrast creates high watch time, comments, and shares.
- **Behavior targeted:** Viral engagement, social sharing, and high ad retention.
- **Where to apply:** The "Derek is cheating" storyline.
