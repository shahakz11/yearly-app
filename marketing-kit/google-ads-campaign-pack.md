## 1. Campaign Architecture & Exact Settings

Operating Google Ads on a hyper-lean budget of **$2.15/day** ($15.00/week) requires a strict "air-tight" defense. A single accidental click on a mobile game or display banner can waste 50% of your daily spend. Every setting below has been calibrated to eliminate wasted spend.

### Campaign Specification Sheet

| Setting | Value | Strategic Rationale |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Campaign Name** | `Yearly - Search - Desktop US CA` | Consistent naming convention (`Product_Channel_Device_Geo`) |
| **Campaign Type** | **Search** (Expert Mode) | Captures high-intent active demand without passive display burn |
| **Daily Budget** | **$2.15 / day** ($15.05 / week) | Strict adherence to the $15/week testing budget |
| **Bidding Strategy** | **Maximize Clicks** | Gathers click volume & algorithmic baseline data without restrictive tCPA caps that stall low budgets |
| **Target CPC Cap** | *None initially* (Leave unchecked) | Allow algorithm to discover natural clearing prices (avg CPC $0.35–$0.70); add \$0.85 cap if CPC spikes |
| **Networks** | **Google Search Network ONLY** | **CRITICAL:** Uncheck *Include Google search partners* AND uncheck *Include Google Display Network* |
| **Locations** | **United States**, **Canada** | Primary high-purchasing power desktop Google Calendar markets |
| **Location Options** | **Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations** | **CRITICAL:** Do NOT select "Presence or interest" (which wastes budget on international clicks) |

| **Devices** | **Desktop / Computers Only** | Mobile phones: **-100%**, Tablets: **-100%**, Computers: **+0%** (Chrome Extension target) |
| **Ad Rotation** | **Optimize: Prefer best performing ads** | Allows Google machine learning to test headline/description RSA combinations |
| **Start Date** | Immediate | Active continuously |
| **Ad Schedule** | **All days, 24 hours** (or 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM EST) | Keeps pacing steady across 7 days |

## 6. Step-by-Step Google Ads Setup Walkthrough (Bypassing Google's Traps)

Google's onboarding interface is designed to push advertisers into automated "Smart" modes and "Performance Max" campaigns that bleed small budgets on low-intent mobile gaming apps and display banners. Follow this exact walkthrough to guarantee a pristine, desktop-only Search campaign.

### Step 1: Switch to "Expert Mode" Immediately
1. Navigate to [ads.google.com](https://ads.google.com).
2. If Google prompts you with "What's your main advertising goal?" or attempts to set up a simplified campaign, scroll to the bottom or click the link that says:  
   **"Switch to Expert Mode"** (or "Are you a professional marketer? Switch to Expert Mode").
3. When prompted to create an account, select **"Create an account without a campaign"** first if you want to configure billing cleanly, or proceed to Step 2.

### Step 2: Choose Campaign Objective
1. Click **+ New Campaign**.
2. When asked to "Select the objective for your campaign", click the tile labeled:  
   **"Create a campaign without a goal's guidance"** *(Trap avoidance: Do NOT select "Brand awareness" or "Leads", which force Smart/PMax recommendations)*.
3. For campaign type, select **Search**.
4. Under "Select the results you want to get from this campaign", check **Website visits** and enter `https://yearly.click`.
5. Enter Campaign Name: `Yearly - Search - Desktop US CA`.
6. Click **Continue**.

### Step 3: Decline "Performance Max" & Bidding Setup
1. If Google shows a modal asking to "Upgrade to Performance Max" or "Try Performance Max", click **Decline / Keep Search**.
2. **Bidding:**
   * "What do you want to focus on?" -> Select **Clicks**.
   * Under "Set a maximum cost per click limit": Leave it **unchecked** initially. With exact/phrase matches and negative lists, our CPC will naturally clear around \$0.40–\$0.70.
   * Customer Acquisition: Leave unchecked.
   * Click **Next**.

### Step 4: Disarm the Networks Traps (CRITICAL)
1. **Networks Section:** Google pre-checks both expansion networks. You MUST uncheck them:
   * ❌ **UNCHECK: "Include Google search partners"** *(Wastes money on low-intent partner portals)*.
   * ❌ **UNCHECK: "Include Google Display Network"** *(High burn rate on non-intent display banners)*.
   * Only **Google Search** should remain active!

### Step 5: Location Targeting & The "Presence" Trap
1. Select **"Enter another location"**.
2. Type and add:
   * **United States** (Country)
   * **Canada** (Country)
3. Click **Location options** (the dropdown below locations).
4. Under **Target:**
   * ❌ *Default (Danger):* "Presence or interest: People in, regularly in, or who've shown interest in your targeted locations"
   * ✅ **SELECT:** **"Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations"**  
   *(Prevents users outside US/Canada who search about US from consuming your clicks).*
5. **Languages:** English.
6. Click **Next**.

### Step 6: Enter Keywords & Build Responsive Search Ads
1. Copy the keywords for **Ad Group 1: Birthday & Anniversary Reminders** from Section 2 into the keyword box.
2. Enter Final URL: `https://yearly.click`.
3. Display paths: `Calendar` / `Reminders`.
4. Paste the 15 Headlines and 4 Descriptions for RSA 1 from Section 2.
5. Create **Ad Group 2: Gifting From Calendar**, paste its keywords, display paths (`Calendar` / `Gifting`), and RSA 2 copy.
6. Click **Next**.

### Step 7: Attach Ad Assets
1. Under **Assets**, add:
   * 4 Sitelinks (from Section 5)
   * 8 Callouts (from Section 5)
   * Structured Snippets (Services: Birthday Reminders, Anniversary Alerts, etc.)
2. Click **Next**.

### Step 8: Set the Daily Budget
1. Google will suggest budgets like \$20/day or \$50/day. Ignore all suggestions.
2. Select **"Set custom budget"**.
3. Enter: **$2.15**.
4. Note that Google will warn: *"Your budget is lower than recommended"*. This is normal for a targeted niche test. Proceed.
5. Click **Next** to review.
6. Click **Publish Campaign**.

### Step 9: The Device Exclusion Gate (-100% Mobile & Tablet Adjustment)
*Immediately after publishing, do NOT skip this step!*

1. In the left navigation menu, click **Campaigns** > select `Yearly - Search - Desktop US CA`.
2. Expand the left sub-menu and click **Devices** (under *Audiences & Demographics* or *More details*).
3. In the Devices table, locate:
   * **Mobile phones** -> Click the pencil icon in the *Bid adj.* column -> Select **Decrease** -> Enter **100%**.
   * **Tablets** -> Click the pencil icon in the *Bid adj.* column -> Select **Decrease** -> Enter **100%**.
   * **Computers** -> Keep at **0%** (or "--").
4. Click **Save**.  
   *Outcome: You are now 100% desktop-only. Not a single cent will be spent on phone searches.*

### Step 10: Turn OFF Auto-Applied Recommendations (The Stealth Google Trap)
*Google quietly opts new accounts into "Auto-apply recommendations", which allows Google's bot to broaden your match types, raise your budgets, or switch your bidding strategy.*

1. In the left menu, click **Recommendations**.
2. In the top right corner of the screen, click the **Auto-apply** icon (looks like a wand with a gear or sparkles).
3. Review the tabs: **Maintain your ads** and **Grow your business**.
4. **UNCHECK ALL BOXES**, specifically:
   * ❌ *Upgrade your keywords to broad match* (Must be OFF)
   * ❌ *Expand your reach with Google Search partners* (Must be OFF)
   * ❌ *Adjust your budgets* (Must be OFF)
   * ❌ *Use optimized targeting* (Must be OFF)
5. Click **Save**.

## 7. Weekly Maintenance Playbook for a $15/Week Account

With a daily budget of \$2.15, managing the campaign takes **5 minutes twice a week**.

### Routine Schedule

| Day | Action | Steps |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Wednesday** | **Search Terms Audit** | Go to **Keywords** > **Search terms**. Look at the actual queries triggered. If any irrelevant query slipped through (e.g., "birthday cake delivery nearby"), check the box and click **Add as negative keyword**. |
| **Sunday** | **Weekly Performance Review** | Check total spend for the week (target: \$15.00 ± \$0.50). Review CTR (healthy target: > 3.5%). Check impressions in Ad Group 1 vs. Ad Group 2. |

### Diagnostic Troubleshooting

* **Issue: 0 Impressions after 48 hours.**  
  *Cause:* Your Maximize Clicks bidding is calibrating, or search volume on exact keywords is temporarily low.  
  *Fix:* Verify that the campaign status is "Eligible" and the desktop bid adjustment is active. Ensure the 10 phrase match keywords are active.
* **Issue: Daily budget spent too quickly (< 10:00 AM).**  
  *Cause:* A popular query is matching aggressively.  
  *Fix:* Review **Search terms**, add any broad terms as exact negative keywords, or set a temporary CPC bid limit of \$0.65 in Campaign Settings > Bidding.
* **Issue: High CTR but low extension installs.**  
  *Cause:* Landing page friction or messaging mismatch.  
  *Fix:* Ensure the landing page hero clearly highlights the "Add to Chrome" button and mentions "Desktop Google Calendar".
