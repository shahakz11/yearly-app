# Product Marketing Context: Yearly (Auto-gifter)

*Last updated: 2026-10-07*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Never miss a milestone. Thoughtful gifts and flowers in one click, right from your calendar.
**What it does:** Yearly automatically scans your calendar for upcoming birthdays, anniversaries, and family milestones, detects the event context, and lets you send curated, fresh flowers or gifts instantly with 1-click checkout before panic or forgetfulness sets in.
**Product category:** Automated Gifting / Relationship Management / Calendar Add-on
**Product type:** Google Workspace Add-on / Browser Extension / Gifting Utility
**Business model:** Free user tool with ~15–20% affiliate margin per bouquet/gift delivery via FloristOne.

**Target companies / segments:** B2C consumers, busy professionals, husbands/partners, adult siblings managing family events.
**Decision-makers:** Busy individuals who value their relationships but suffer from cognitive overload, forgetfulness ("שכחנות"), or procrastination ("עצלנות").
**Primary use case:** Zero-friction milestone rescue — sending gifts and flowers in seconds without leaving calendar/chat or stressing over gift shopping.

**Use cases:**

- *Sibling MVP:* Family chat panics about parents' anniversary; one sibling taps once and replies "It's handled."
- *Surprise Innocence:* Unexpected floral delivery arrives at the doorstep, transforming panic or false suspicion into romantic admiration.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** People deeply care about their partners and families, but human memory fails ("שכחנות"), schedules get hectic, and the friction of ordering gifts leads to procrastination ("עצלנות") and forgotten milestones.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- *Standard calendar reminders:* Only tell you it's an anniversary; they don't solve the gift buying, leaving you frantically googling local florists 2 hours before dinner.
- *Traditional flower websites:* Overwhelming 50-page catalogs, surprise \$25 delivery fees at step 5, tedious 10-field address forms.
- *Personal assistants:* Expensive and invasive.
**What it costs them:** Ruined anniversaries, emotional distance, guilt, awkward family chats, emergency \$150 grocery store wilted roses.
**Emotional tension:** Panic ("Oh crap, today is the 12th?!"), guilt, fear of disappointing loved ones, feeling like a bad partner.

---

## Competitive Landscape
- **Direct:** Traditional Florist delivery sites (1-800-Flowers, ProFlowers, Bouqs) — fall short because they require proactive planning, complex search, and tedious checkout friction.
- **Secondary:** Calendar alerts & To-Do apps (Apple Reminders, Todoist, Google Calendar alerts) — fall short because they create a task instead of completing the task.
- **Indirect:** Emergency grocery runs, last-minute gift cards — fall short because they feel impersonal and low-effort.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Calendar-native awareness: Detects events right where your schedule lives.
- Frictionless 1-Click checkout: Curated top-performing arrangements pre-loaded.
- "Saved at the buzzer" speed: Zero cognitive fatigue, under 10 seconds from reminder to confirmed order.
**Why customers choose us:** We turn pure forgetfulness/laziness into effortless heroic thoughtfulness.
